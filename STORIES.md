# Stories

Short notes for this POC only. Not Protostar. Not a baseball lab.

## The pad is the ship

You do not tap a button and then wait for a heading. The circle is already there, fat enough for a thumb, sitting above the notch and the home bar. You drag. The dart points where you pushed and the world slides. That is the whole sentence.

## Coast is a kind of quiet

Let go and the burn dies, but the ship does not. Drag is light on purpose. You slide past the clay world, then the ice one, and the nose slowly agrees with the path you are already on. The interesting part is the half-second after you release.

## A planet is a suggestion

The three balls are scenery with a little pull and a soft bounce. You can ignore them. You can clip one and come off at a new angle. You can lean the stick against the tug and draw a lazy curve. Nothing to dock with. Nothing to buy. Just enough mass that empty space has a grain.
